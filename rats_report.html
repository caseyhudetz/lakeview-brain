Persistent, or just big?

Rodent complaints on 165 blocks over 68 months. The blocks that look chronically infested are the blocks with the most complaints — and once you divide volume out, almost nothing is left.

The finding

Ranking blocks by share of months with a complaint produces almost exactly the same list as ranking them by raw total. The two are Spearman +0.93.

That is not a coincidence, it is arithmetic: a block with 6 complaints cannot appear in 40 different months. Persistence is bounded above by volume, so on a skewed dataset it mostly re-measures size.

Spikiness fails the same way from the other end: it is -0.50 against volume, so it just finds small blocks. The two lists share no blocks at all — but that disjointness is an artifact of measuring size from opposite ends, not evidence of two real signals.

The fix, and what it leaves

Take each block's own complaint count and scatter it at random across the months, weighted by the neighbourhood's seasonal profile. That gives the number of distinct months the block should occupy. The standardised gap between actual and expected is a spread score with volume removed.

Every block, adjusted

No block in East Lakeview is meaningfully more spread out than its own volume predicts. There is no steady-drip block. There are, however, several genuinely episodic ones — and those are real.

Method

Data
Chicago 311 service requests, dataset v6vf-nfxy (the post-2018 schema). Rodent Baiting/Rat Complaint only — matching %RAT% also catches Inaccurate Fuel Pump and Retail Scales complaints, so the type filter matches whole words.
Geography
Community area 6, then a bounding box for East Lakeview: Diversey to Irving Park, Halsted to the lake. 8,335 of the 11,661 Lake View rows fell outside it.
Blocks
Street number floored to the hundred, joined to direction and name. Street type is folded in, because Broadway appears both with and without a suffix and the split deflates exactly the score in question. 5 rows had no usable address.
Duplicates
187 of 3,321 rows carry the city's duplicate flag (5.6%). Collapsing them leaves the persistence top fifteen unchanged, so the conclusion does not depend on the choice.
Null model
Occupancy with unequal cell probabilities: for a block with n complaints, E[K] = Σ(1 − qⱼ) where qⱼ = (1 − pⱼ)ⁿ and pⱼ is month j's neighbourhood-wide share. Variance includes the pairwise covariance term. Both mean and variance were checked against Monte Carlo before use and agree to three decimals.

What would change this

The boundary is a guess
The bounding box is mine, not the map's. It discards 8,335 Lake View rows. A different definition of East Lakeview yields a different block list — this is the largest single source of error here.
Multiple comparisons
55 blocks were scored. At that count a couple of |z| > 2 are expected by chance. Treat the −3s and −4s as real and the −2s as suggestive.
Complaints are not rats
This measures reporting behaviour. A block with an organised block club and a block with the same rats and no one calling look nothing alike here.
